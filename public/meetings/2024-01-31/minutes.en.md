## Taking out a loan for the paving works

The PAVE works are underway for 466,920 euros (including study costs) of which 10,605 have already been paid. There therefore remains to be covered 456,315 euros.

Subsidies have been awarded for 354,350 euros, of which 90,000 have already been paid. (60,000 under the fines, and 30,000 on the DSIL 2020). There would still be 264,350 euros to be received after completion of the works. It therefore falls to the municipality to cover its share by a loan.

It is proposed to take out a loan of 210,000 euros.

The following proposal was made by Crédit Agricole

The municipal council votes by twelve votes for and one vote against.

The taking out of the loan with Crédit Agricole on the basis of a duration of 15 years - The authorisation to B. LAURET, to sign the loan contract
