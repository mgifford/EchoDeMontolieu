## Closure of the PLU project before the start of consultations

Bernard Lauret indicates that in view of the current agitation surrounding the sole urban extensions included in the document, he has decided that there will be no vote to close the project before the next elections.

He considers it more appropriate to leave the care of this vote to the next council, which will act as it wishes. He specifies that given the incompressible deadlines for consultations, and the following summer period, voting now, or in a month and a half, will change nothing, and that the definitive closure of the PLU will in any case take place at the end of 2026. Laure ESCARÉ shares the results of the consultations with the councillors, following the proposals from the last public meeting, the requests for clarification from SOLIHA and the latest requests for modification of the PLU.

- Widening of the plots on the OAP of Soil No. 2 – this widening leads to a

shortening of the lengths in order not to consume more land, but which could be compensated by the sale in the back of the garden of non-buildable land. It should be noted that the presentation of the OAP only concerns the buildable part of the land. This request was accepted by 8 councillors out of 10 who expressed themselves, 2 abstained

- For La Ferrière, increase of the land strip from 12 to 15 meters and absence

of opening at the back of the garden onto the wooded strip.

This request was rejected by 8 councillors out of 10 who expressed themselves, 2 abstained. The increase in the strip reduced the surface area of the plots by as much, which could not be compensated on the other side since the road closed the operation. Furthermore, the absence of opening of the back gardens onto this strip created a discontinuity in the urban extension operations, contrary to the application of the mountain law.

- For the OAP 2 of the Soil, was it necessary to impose a ridge orientation, or to leave the latter

free to facilitate the installation of constructions according to the plots? 8 councillors out of 11 voters chose a free orientation (it should be noted that the orientation of the ridges was imposed on OAP No. 1, in order to preserve its integration into the landscape)

- For the OAP of La Ferrière, did the council want the 2 previous OAPs to be

entirely completed before authorising the start of the OAP of La Ferrière, or did he want to retain a realisation as a percentage of the OAP 2 of Le Sol for this start? As a reminder, the OAP of Le SOL n°2 can only start when the first is realised at 60% 8 councillors out of 11 voters asked that the OAP 2 of Le Sol be entirely completed, before the one of La Ferrière could start.

Jeanne ETORÉ-LORTHOLARY intervenes to contest this method of consultation, which, if it can be useful and legal in cases of emergency to facilitate the sale of a property in the absence of a council meeting, seems unsuitable in this case. These points should have been submitted for deliberation within a council.

Laure ESCARÉ indicates that these points were necessary to allow the provider to work and finalise the documents, which were received on 5 March at the town hall.

Elle précise que deux OAP ont été ajoutées : l’une pour le cimetière et l’autre pour la zone d’équipement au sud du Sol. En effet, pour toutes les zones AU, y compris les zones AUE, les OAP sont obligatoires. [p.7](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/CRCM-09-03-2026-site-internet.pdf#page=7)

L’ensemble des documents a été transmis aux conseillers, une édition a été réalisée, qui est mise à disposition des montolivains, dans l’attente d’un prochain vote.

## Demande d’avance sur subvention pour l’association trail st roch

Monsieur Thierry BOST, président de l’association Trail St Roch a sollicité une avance sur la subvention qui pourrait être attribuée par le prochain conseil dans le cadre de son budget 2026. Bernard LAURET interroge les conseillers sur l’accord de principe de versement d’une avance. Le conseil donne son accord à l’unanimité.

Sur le montant à accorder, il est précisé que la demande de subvention elle-même est de 400 euros. Le nouveau conseil ne votant pas le budget avant la mi-avril (présentation et quinze jours de latence avant le vote), il ne peut être envisagé de porter l’avance au montant de la demande actuelle. Laure ESCARÉ indique que pour l’année précédente, la subvention avait été accordée à l’association des parents d’élève pour l’organisation du trail pour un montant de 300 €. Monsieur Thierry BOST, sollicité par le conseil, précise que l’intégralité des bénéfices sera reversée à l’APE comme l’année précédente.

Après échange, le conseil vote à l’unanimité l’octroi d’une avance sur subvention de 300 euros.
