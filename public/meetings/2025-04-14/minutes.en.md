## Of the deployment of the low frequency network

The Mayor informs the Municipal Council that as part of the deployment of a low-speed network in the department of Aude, it would be appropriate to sign an accommodation agreement with the Audois Union of Energies and Digital (SYADEN). The relay concerned is located in the locality (lieu-dit) “Montpertus”.

This agreement is intended to define the conditions under which SYADEN is authorised to:

- Occupy the public domain of the municipality,
- To welcome and have the station operated by an infrastructure operator, [p.2](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/CRCM-14-AVRIL-2025-site-internet.pdf#page=2)
- To welcome the technical equipment on the site, this in order to limit the number of Internet

transmission stations and to preserve the integrity of the landscape.

The draft agreement was sent with the summons to this council

The council votes unanimously to authorize the Mayor to sign the hosting agreement with the SYADEN for the land located in the locality (lieu-dit) “Montpertus”.

### Point sur la fongibilité des crédits [p.6](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/CRCM-14-AVRIL-2025-site-internet.pdf#page=6)

La municipality having adopted the M57 framework, it can benefit from easing measures on the budgetary plan and in particular from the principle of credit fungibility. On the occasion of the budget vote, the council can authorise the Mayor to carry out credit transfers from chapter to chapter for the financial year in question. These credit transfers between chapters (excluding personnel expenses and expenses for order) cannot exceed 7.5 % of the initial consolidated budget of budget amendment (décision modificative). This percentage may be different for each of the two operating or investment sections.

Jeanne ETORÉ-LORTHOLARY indicates that she is against this way of operating. Laure ESCARÉ indicates that this flexibility, only allows to avoid convening the council for a budget amendment (décision modificative) when funds are lacking in one chapter, while they are available in another.

Laure ESCARÉ specifies that these transfers must be the subject of an express decision by the Mayor, subject to the obligation of transmission to the representative of the State, responsible for legality control. They are also transmitted to the public accountant, and the Mayor is required to inform the council during its next meeting.

The council votes the general budget with 11 votes for and 3 votes against. The council authorizes the Mayor to proceed with credit transfers from chapter to chapter for the financial year to which it relates, within the limit of 7.5% of the initial consolidated budget of amending decisions, for the two sections with 12 votes for and 2 against.
