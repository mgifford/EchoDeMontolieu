## Summary
The municipal council of 27 November 2025 approved the minutes of the previous session, examined the adjustments to the draft PLU, notably on the areas of La Ferrière and Le Sol, and voted on several budgetary decisions, including a corrective budget for 2025, the renewal of the treasury line and expenditure authorisations for 2026. Decisions were taken concerning the financing of the swimming pool and road works, as well as an advance on a grant for an association and the creation of a working group on tourist signage. Opinions were given on sales of private property.

## Points discussed

- **Local urbanism plan before consultation phase**: It is planned to add a wooded strip to the OAP of La Ferrière and to move the Brétous development to an equivalent land, possibly behind the OAP of Le Sol. - **Request for a grant from Carcassonne Agglo for the swimming pool works**: Following the refusal of a grant from the region, the council votes in favour of a request for a grant from Carcassonne Agglo for works estimated at 244 228.07 € HT. Vote: unanimously. [p.2-3]
- **Financing plan for road works and integration into the 2025 budget**: The works on the Barrens, Moulin de Cathala and Tannerie paths are financed with a departmental grant of 6 224.60 € and own funds, including 18 335.00 € from the FPIC 2025. The plan is integrated into the corrective budget 2025 for implementation in early 2026. Vote: 12 votes for, 2 votes against. [p.3]
