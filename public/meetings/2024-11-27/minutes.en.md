## Agreement with the SYADEN

The work carried out on the public electricity distribution network may involve interventions on other networks such as public lighting networks and/or passive infrastructure intended to accommodate electronic communications networks (IPCE).

These various networks may fall under several project owners: o The SYADEN for the electricity distribution network and the IPCE, o The local authority for public lighting works.

If this is the case, the SYADEN may be designated as the sole project owner for all the work. In the context of the renovation of the Ceres Franco museum, a reinforcement operation on the electricity distribution network is necessary, an operation also affecting public lighting. To this end, an agreement must be signed between the SYADEN and the municipality, an agreement including a financial component, part of the costs falling to the village for public lighting. The agreement was transmitted to the council in advance, which is called upon to vote to authorise the Mayor to sign the agreement, and to complete the subsidy application (representing 50 % of the cost to be borne by the municipality). The amount to be borne by the municipality will be entered in the amending budget.

- The council, unanimously, authorises the Mayor to sign the agreement and make the subsidy application [p.2](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/CRCM-27-11-2024-site-internet.pdf#page=2)

relating to it.

## Amending budget

In accordance with the presentation made to the finance committee, the operating budget is adjusted on two chapters, that of personnel costs and related expenses, and that of other current management expenses. The total remains the same, the difference being taken from the transfer to the investment section. For the first chapter, that of personnel costs, it is necessary to cover the replacements required on the one hand by the provision of personnel as an ASEM from January to the beginning of July, and on the other hand by sick leave, and seasonal contracts. Furthermore, the assessment of social security charges must also be revised upwards.

A portion of sick leave is offset by revenue, but most often with a delay. (in this respect, a little more than 36,000 euros are recorded as revenue)

Regarding current operating expenses, it is about releasing the necessary means to help MVDL to finish the year via a complementary grant of 3500 euros, and adjusting some accounts including that of the fire service which has increased year on year.

## Information on the cleaning of tags [p.6](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/CRCM-27-11-2024-site-internet.pdf#page=6)

Laure ESCARÉ reads the letter from Marie-Hélène ROBIN questioning the town hall about the fact that the tags have not yet been erased, and about what the municipality intends to do about it. Bernard LAURET recalls the sequence of events: noting the facts on Friday, filing a complaint by the town hall the following Sunday, with the chance that the perpetrator had been identified. The municipality joined the civil action, and the responsible person was convicted. Laure ESCARÉ indicates that the court has not sent anything to date, that the secretariat has called the lawyer of the opposing party in vain and that neither the latter, nor the perpetrator, both contacted by email, have given any sign of life to date.

The company retained for the cleaning, contacted today, has no availability before January. Consequently, it was decided that the municipal services would clean the public walls as best as possible, and that the company would intervene to complete the work if traces remained. Jeanne ETORÉ-LORTHOLARY asks if there are no other companies capable of doing the work. Laure ESCARÉ will investigate if this is the case, but hopes that the products used by the municipal services will be sufficiently effective in the immediate future.

It is asked if the other walls belonging to private individuals could also be cleaned, notably those of the Mallast pizzeria.

Bernard LAURET indicates that municipal employees are not to touch walls belonging to private individuals, especially if the latter have filed a complaint. It is up to them to make the decision themselves. The anti-graffiti products used by the technical team can always be recommended to them. A dozen emails received this morning at the town hall report a response from the Ministry of the Interior of 30 August 1990, which, on the basis of article 24, paragraph 4 of the law of 29 July 1881 on the freedom of the press, instructs the mayor to remove “officially graffiti of a racist, anti-Semitic or directly inciting to murder, the content of which falls under criminal law”, however it is recalled that the judgment acquitted the author of the offence of “public provocation to hatred and violence based on origin, race or religion”. Consequently, the argument presented cannot apply, whatever one may think of the racist or other nature of the tags.
