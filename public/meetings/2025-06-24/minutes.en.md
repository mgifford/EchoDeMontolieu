## Arbitration concerning the PLU regulations

The Mayor indicates that 4 points are to be studied.

The first concerns the rate of implementation of the priority OAP from which it is possible to launch the second operation.

It is necessary beforehand to name the OAPs, in order to disconnect their number from their order of prioritization.

Currently the OAP N°1 is that of La Ferrière, and is the last in order of priority.

It is proposed to name the OAPs as follows:

OAP Les Brétous (the first in terms of priority)

### OAP du Sol

### OAP La Ferrière

The council unanimously validates the 3 names of the OAPs

Laure ESCARÉ recalls that the second OAP cannot be launched until the realisation rate of the first is reached. This rate is understood as the realisation of the constructions. The Council cannot fall below 50 %, but it can be positioned higher, which is more acceptable to institutional partners.

A rate of 60 % is proposed

The council unanimously sets the realisation rate at 60 %

The second point concerns the list of elements to be protected under the small heritage. Soliha has transmitted a mapping of the elements (marked with stars), supplemented by the sending of a document by Jeanne ETORÉ-LORTHOLARY. The mapping does not include everything, the list of Mme ETORÉ-LORTHOLARY, having been received after the establishment. [p.3](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CRCM-24062025-site-internet.pdf#page=3)

Après échange il est proposé de refaire une réunion de travail afin de valider ou pas chaque proposition. La date du 1er juillet est retenue. La réunion se tiendra à 20 h. \_[not translated: an automatic check failed]\_

The third point concerns the orientation of the ridges for the OAPs. As with the OAP La Ferrière, the orientation of the ridges is not parallel to the street, the rule of ridge orientation initially provided for in the regulations of the areas to be Urbanised, has been removed (since it provides for this parallel orientation to the street). Regulating the orientation of the ridges is not an obligation.

After discussion, it is unanimously voted to ask Soliha to have a regulation specific to each OAP.

The fourth point concerns the regulation of farm camping. Following the questioning of farmers or breeders on their farm camping project, two requests have been received, one concerning a motorhome area, the other the installation of a tiny-house.

The council can choose to include in the regulations in a general way (in article 2 of the authorised occupations of the Agricultural zone) this principle, or to open this possibility within the framework of two STECAL (Sectors of Size and Limited Reception Capacity) corresponding to the two requests. In this case there will be a special zoning, as was created for the mini farm, for the tiny-house. The advantage of the STECAL is to avoid the proliferation of projects throughout the entire zone A, and to stick strictly to the requests expressed. Éric OLIVIER points out that farmers have been given until 30 June to make their projects known, there are still a few days left for that.

Laure ESCARÉ recognises it but if there are other requests, the question must be dealt with as for the first two: a specific agreement for each, with a STECAL which limits the application of the PLU to the known projects only, or a wider opening.

Jeanne ETORÉ-LORTHOLARY does not see why a wider opening could not be possible.

Éric OLIVIER points out that if only two requests have been received to date, one may have, during a farm sale, a new operator who may wish to do farm camping, and if this is not provided for in the regulations, it will be necessary to intervene by a revision of the PLU. Jacques SAFONT asks if it could not be provided for globally in the regulations subject to the agreement of the town hall, in order to avoid too many projects, and of too large a size. Laure ESCARÉ specifies that these installations must already have the agreement of the chamber of agriculture, and they are already subject to strict regulations, for example a parking area for camping cars must not exceed 5 to 6 pitches. If we want to avoid proliferation, the STECAL allows to limit by creating a specific zoning.

After discussion, it was agreed that SOLIHA would be consulted to further investigate the question: is it possible to include in the regulations an opening, with the agreement of the municipality for projects.

## Commitment to the mutual steps of carcassonne agglo

The Public Procurement Code allows a plurality of public bodies justifying common needs related to a determined purchase, to associate in procurement groups in order to optimise advantages both at the economic level and at the level of the quality of services. The objective is to allow all members of the group to benefit from the same financial conditions for the provision of identical services, which will also have the advantage of pooling the market award procedures.

During a previous council meeting the municipality wished to associate with the formation of permanent groups for:

- Services of assistance and legal advice
- Topographical services
- Periodic verification services (electrical compliance, SSI, fire-fighting equipment, areas

of games and sports equipment, machines, ladders, PPE, marquee, tent and structure, asbestos diagnosis before work, DPE, indoor air control ERP, RVRAT)

The operating conditions of these groups are fixed by the various agreements which have been transmitted upstream to the councillors.

### The council unanimously

- Approves the adhesion of the municipality of MONTOLIEU to the procurement groups

permanent for:

- Services of assistance and legal advice
- Topographical services
- Periodic verification services
- Approves the terms of the agreements establishing the procurement groups annexed to

this deliberation,

- Accepts that the Carcassonne Agglo Community of Municipalities be the coordinator of the

grouping,

- Authorises the Mayor to sign the membership form (appendix 1) for each grouping

and to complete appendix 2,

- Authorises the Mayor to ensure the execution of the corresponding contracts, amendments, for the

specific needs of the municipality.

## Boulodrome development

The lighting has been requested for several years by the pétanque club. An opportunity to recover poles having arisen, development is possible. A quote to obtain compliant and secure lighting quickly was sent for an amount of 9685.20 euros.

The company JARIE has already intervened to install and seal the poles (for 1320 €). It is proposed to put this operation into investment (as for the stadium lighting). The municipality will be able to recover the VAT, and call on the FPIC.

The council votes unanimously for the registration of the necessary work in the investment section.

## Renouvellement du contrat d’assurance de la commune [p.6](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/CRCM-24062025-site-internet.pdf#page=6)

Le contrat d’assurance de la commune s’achève au 31 décembre 2025, il convient donc de procéder à son renouvellement.

Compte tenu de la technicité de ce type de contrat, et des difficultés à apprécier les offres qui pourraient être faites, il est proposé au conseil de faire appel à un courtier en assurance. Le contrat doit couvrir plusieurs types de dommages - Dommages aux Véhicules - Dommages aux Biens - Protection juridique, Protection fonctionnelle, Responsabilités A la question du choix du courtier Bernard LAURET indique que l’agence immobilière AVEROUX offre aussi ce type de service. Elle sera contactée à cet effet.

Le conseil vote à l’unanimité le recours à un courtier en assurance pour le renouvellement des contrats d’assurances pour la commune.

## Demande de subvention exceptionnelle de la cooperative scolaire

La coopérative scolaire a sollicité en février dernier une subvention exceptionnelle de 380 euros, afin de permettre à tous les enfants de profiter d’un séjour en Ariège en fin d’année scolaire. Cette demande n’avait pas été rapportée, et lors du vote des subventions, aucune alerte n’a été faite. Une relance de la sollicitation a donc été faite au secrétariat. La demande est en conséquence présentée ce jour par Bernard Lauret qui en fait la lecture. Le conseil vote par 13 voix pour, et une contre la subvention exceptionnelle de 380 euros à la coopérative scolaire.

## Reunion de la commission communale des impots directs (ccid)

Dans le cadre de la maison référencée au cadastre AB 0341, il s’avère que la mairie est en indivision depuis 1969, avec une personne dont il n’y a plus de nouvelle depuis longtemps, la commune ayant toujours payé l’entièreté de la taxe foncière.

La commune a donc la possibilité d’acquérir la pleine propriété au titre des « biens supposés sans maitre ».

Pour se faire, il est nécessaire de réunir la CCID qui doit valider le document du Trésor Public, justifiant que la taxe foncière n’est plus payée depuis au moins 3 ans, ou est payée par un tiers.

A ce titre les membres de cette commission seront appelés à se réunir sous la présidence Du maire ou de son adjoint délégué, en cas d’absence, les suppléants assureront le remplacement.

Les commissaires titulaires sont les suivants :

AGASSE Cécile, BOST BERGE Magali, ETORE LORTHOLARY Jeanne, MONRAISIN Valérie PICQUE Vincent, BARBERO Aurélie Les commissaires suppléants sont les suivants :

CASAS Antoine, GHARBI Jamel, OLIVIER Eric, SAFONT Jacques, BOST Thierry, OLIVIER Jean- Luc Après échange, il est convenu de réunir la CCID le 1er juillet juste avant la réunion de travail sur les PLU.
