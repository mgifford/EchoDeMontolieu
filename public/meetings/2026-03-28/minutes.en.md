The Mayor takes the chair for the remainder of the meeting.

## Setting the number of deputy mayors

The Mayor recalls that the municipal council must determine the number of deputy mayors without that number exceeding 30% of the legal size of the municipal council, which is 15. Therefore, he proposes to set the number of deputy mayors at 4.

The council approves unanimously.

## Election of deputy mayors

The Mayor proposes a list of deputies composed as follows:

- Mr. LABESSEDE René 2. Mrs. BARBERO Aurélie 3. Mr. BOST Thierry 4. Mrs PAUTOU

### Agnès

The Mayor asks if there is another list of candidates, Mrs. Jeanne Etoré-Lortholary puts herself forward as a candidate. It is replied to her that she must present a list of deputies. She replies that she is therefore a candidate for nothing.

Jeanne Etoré-Lortholary insists that it be noted in the minutes that the elected officials of the Agir ensemble autrement pour Montolieu list, while acknowledging their heavy defeat, considering that they nevertheless represented nearly 40% of the Montolieu voters, had requested a position as deputy for culture and the position delegated to the agglomeration community, that Mr. Jacques Safont accompanied by Mrs. Agnès Pautou had come to inform him of the rejection of this request in person at his home.

The Mayor invites the councillors to go to the polling booth to proceed with the vote. Result of the council vote:

## Reading of the local elected official's charter

The Mayor proceeds to read the local elected official's charter:

1 In the exercise of their term of office, the local elected official undertakes to respect the principles of liberty, equality, fraternity and secularism as well as the laws and symbols of the Republic. 2 The local elected official exercises their functions with impartiality, diligence, dignity, probity and integrity. Within this framework, they pursue only the general interest, to the exclusion of any interest that is personal to them, directly or indirectly, or of any other particular interest. 3 The local elected official ensures that any conflict of interest prohibited by law is prevented or immediately stopped. When their personal interests are involved in matters brought before the deliberative body of which they are a member, the local elected official undertakes to disclose them before the debate and the vote. 4 The local elected official undertakes not to use for other purposes the resources and means made available to them for the exercise of their term of office or functions. 5 In the exercise of their functions, the local elected official refrains from taking measures granting them a personal or professional advantage. 6 The local elected official participates assiduously in the meetings of the deliberative body and the bodies in which they have been appointed. 7 Having been elected by universal suffrage, the local elected official is and remains accountable for their actions for the duration of their term of office to all the citizens of the territorial collectivity, to whom they report on the acts and decisions taken in the exercise of their functions. 8 The local elected official declares, in a register kept by the territorial collectivity, donations, benefits and invitations with a value that they estimate to be greater than 150 euros which they have received by reason of their term of office. This declaration requirement does not apply to customary gifts and travel undertaken at the invitation of French public authorities or as part of another elective mandate. 9 Local elected officials may benefit from the payment of an allowance for the effective exercise of their elective functions and the reimbursement of expenses incurred in this context, in the conditions provided for by law. 10 Local elected officials are affiliated, for the exercise of their term of office, to the general social security scheme in the conditions defined in article L 382-31 of the social security code and to special schemes defined by the general code of local authorities. 11 Local elected officials benefit, in the course of their functions, from protection organised by the territorial collectivity, in accordance with the rules laid down by the penal code, special laws and the general code of local authorities. 12 The right to training is recognised for local elected officials. It is exercised in the conditions laid down by the general code of local authorities. 13 Any person holding a local mandate benefits, in conditions provided for by law, from guarantees granted during the exercise of the mandate and at its end and allowing in particular to reconcile it with a professional activity or the pursuit of higher education. 14 Any local elected official may consult a ethics advisor responsible for providing them with any useful advice for respecting the principles mentioned in article L. 1111-13 of the general code of local authorities. A decree of the Council of State determines the procedures and criteria for the designation of ethics advisors.

The Mayor hands a copy to each councillor for signature. [p.3](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/CRCM-28-mars-2026-site-internet.pdf#page=3)
