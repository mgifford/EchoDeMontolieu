## Approval of the plan d’amenagement et de developpement durable (PADD)

VERSION N°5

As a reminder, following the second public meeting and the requests for modification that followed, the service provider company proceeded with a recalculation of the population objectives and a modification of the mapping, at the request of the council.

The OAP planned on Les Brétous was abandoned and transferred to a second OAP at Le Sol. The number of new constructions went from 21 to 19, in order to loosen the density while remaining within the SCOT standards.

The new version of the PADD, with the modifications made in red, was sent to the councillors on December 29th last, then again on January 7th, with the correction of page 7: the mapping of the areas of urban expansion still contained in its first version an area encompassing both Les Brétous (abandoned project) and Le Sol. The area was modified to only cover Le Sol.

A 3rd public meeting will be proposed to the population on January 27th next.

The following clarifications are provided to the councillors. [p.2](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CRCM-8-01-2026-site-internet.pdf#page=2)

Concerning the OAP of La Ferrière, and after discussion and site visit with members of the collective, the proposal issued, after consultation with Soliha is as follows: - Reduction from 6 constructions to 5 constructions - A 15 meter wooded strip - Then the back gardens of the villas - The road serving the subdivision in one way will pass on the opposite side of what was originally planned, i.e. at the bottom of the plot and not along the already built land, and will join the street by crossing the current pine tree area, and not along Mrs. Cappiot's plot.

Laure ESCARÉ specifies that to respect the densification obligations due to the implementation of the 15-meter band (which would represent 1000 m²), a non-used reserve of 600 m² is mobilized, and that on OAP 2 of the Sol, the surface area of the buildable plots would be reduced (2 to 4 meters approximately, calculations to be finalized) to gain the missing 400 m². However, to avoid plots that are too small, it will always be possible for the owner to sell a complementary part of his land but at the price of non-buildable land. The buyers will be obliged to use only the buildable part for the construction of their house, but for all that the plots will be more spacious.

A message was left after the site visit to Soliha to adapt the presentation of the OAPs.

On this subject and for OAP n°2 of the Sol, Mrs. Labastie asked if the council wished the houses to be aligned. There was no complete feedback from the councilors. After discussion, the council decides that beyond the orientation which must be the same, the constructions can be positioned freely in the buildable part of the plots.

Once the vote on this version of the PADD, the municipality must respect a delay of two months before approving the PLU project.

The council approves by 12 votes for, one against and one abstention the presented PADD.

## Request for advance on grant for the world days of art

Within the framework of the organization of the world days of art, the collective submitted a request for an advance on grant of an amount of 200 euros.

In 2025, the grant had been supported by MVdL, because the municipality could not allocate any

subvention en dehors du cadre juridique d’une association. [p.3](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CRCM-8-01-2026-site-internet.pdf#page=3)

Pour 2026, la demande sera portée par l’association la Manuf qui participe elle-même à l’évènement.

Monsieur le Maire donne lecture de la lettre de demande déposée en mairie par le collectif La demande de subvention est de 500 euros, mais il est juste sollicité une avance équivalente à ce qui a été accordée l’année précédente, charge pour le nouveau conseil élu en mars prochain de statuer sur le montant total de la subvention.

Le conseil accorde à l’unanimité une avance de 200 euros pour l’organisation des journées mondiales de l’art. Cette avance sera versée à l’association la Manuf.
