## Opinion on the framework document of the Aude regulating the installation of ground-mounted solar power plants [p.2](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/CRCM-27-05-2025-site-internet.pdf#page=2)

A meeting took place on 20 May, during which the plots open to the installation of ground-mounted solar power plants were examined, according to the study transmitted by the Chamber of Agriculture to the Prefecture, which led to the establishment of the framework document for the Aude. Following this meeting, a draft opinion was drawn up and sent to the councillors on 26 May. For those who did not have time to read it, it is read out. Laure ESCARÉ indicates that a complementary filter concerns the ZAENR for 3 plots, it concerns the qualification of forest area. She will document this point to complete the opinion. Jeanne ETORÉ-LORTHOLARY indicates that the document retains land that is quite visible from Saint-Roch, which we cannot approve, and land on the edge of the departmental road. However, it excludes areas located in Zones of Natural Interest for Ecology, Fauna and Flora (ZNIEFF) for the installation of photovoltaic power plants, which seems quite logical (even if it is not legally imperative), so she cannot subscribe to the arguments developed on behalf of the Council.

The council adopts the opinion proposed by 13 votes in favour and one abstention

## Issues to be decided in the context of the PLU works

On 12 May, a document was sent to the councillors and Soliha, outlining the points to be corrected following the re-reading of the draft regulations in its second version. Some points are to be decided by the council.

- swimming pool area: while the question of water is going to become increasingly pressing,

should we limit the area of swimming pools?

After discussion, the council decides unanimously to limit the surface area of swimming pools to 32 m²

- for the UAa zone (old village centre) – currently it is marked “roller shutters

are prohibited” to be replaced by “roller shutters are authorised insofar as there is no co-visibility with a listed building, and subject to the express conditions that the housings are integrated into the construction, that they are the same colour as the rest of the woodwork and that the wooden slats are retained to respect the architectural arrangement of the façade”

Jeanne ETORÉ-LORTHOLARY explains that the generalisation of roller shutters would completely distort the urban landscape, and the fact that individuals have illegally installed roller shutters is not a reason to legitimise their installation. If we want to preserve the character and appeal of the old village centre, we must also preserve the elements that make up that character, in particular the genoise windows and the three-pane windows with two-leaf shutters. This has nothing to do with the notion of co-visibility, which applies to the protection of a historic monument and its surroundings, but says nothing about the coherence of an urban environment.

Laure ESCARÉ indicates that when applying for the regularisation of the installation of a roller shutter, the ABF introduced this notion of co-visibility, and integration into the construction, leaving the mayor the choice of following or not its opinion. It might be appropriate to take this nuance into account in the PLU regulations.

Jeanne ETORÉ-LORTHOLARY believes that we must accept the advantages and disadvantages of living in the heart of the village: proximity to shops, but parking difficulties and respect for architectural heritage. The municipality should be more rigorous in monitoring these drifts, and not simply send a registered letter to people it dislikes.

Bernard LAURET replies that this does not solve the problem of what has already been done, the best thing would be to open up the possibility of regularisation. [p.3](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/CRCM-27-05-2025-site-internet.pdf#page=3)

Jean Luc OLIVIER believes that the municipality should prosecute the people concerned, at least once to have an effect, and to make it known.

Bernard LAURET recalls that the ABF authorised it for the EPHAD.

After discussion, the council decides to maintain the initial version by 8 votes for, 4 votes against and 2 abstentions

- Percentage and prioritisation of OAP

The DTTM is attentive to ensuring that the OAP are prioritised. Consequently, the following order had been retained - First, the one located on the Bretous, - Second, the one located behind La Ferrière - Third, the one located along the greenway, facing the cemetery. The draft PPRI currently under consultation with the residents of the municipality, has been transmitted to SOLIHA who noted that the OAP n°2 is affected by a runoff hazard, which implies fixing in the regulations an increase in floor levels. The final document of the PPRI being expected at best in May-June 2026, it is recommended to modify the order of priority of the OAP, and to position the one of La Ferrière last. Indeed, the last OAP requires a revision of the PLU to be implemented, which will allow at the time of adjustment the regulations of the PLU to the requirements of the PPRI.

The municipal council votes unanimously for the new order of the OAP: - First, the one located on the Bretous, - Second, the one located along the greenway, facing the cemetery - Third, the one located behind La Ferrière.

To be able to implement the second OAP, the PLU regulations provide for the implementation of 80 % of the first. Soliha was questioned to know if this percentage was an obligation or if it could be lowered, in case of difficulty in the implementation of a priority OAP.

After discussion, the council unanimously decides to have the notion of implementation (purchase? construction?) clarified and, depending on this, to review this point with the recommendations of our provider (is 50 % acceptable?).

## Grant application from the association Le rêve du martinet

The association reiterated its request for a subsidy, following exchanges between it, the municipality and the MVDL association to which the applicant had been referred. Mr BARDOUT specified in an email the position of MVdL, which the Mayor reads aloud.

Jeanne ETORÉ-LORTHOLARY specifies that it has nothing against the activities of Le Rêve du Martinet (in which it has willingly participated) nor against the actors in question.

It nevertheless finds the method, consisting, after the vote of the subsidy budget, of “coming back to the charge” and reiterating a request to which an answer has already been given, rather unpleasant.

Le Rêve du Martinet proposes activities (essentially conferences, exhibitions) rigorously comparable to those proposed by MVdL, but without any promotional objective (the mention Village du Livre does not even appear on the posters), rather in a spirit of regular and friendly meetings, without any well-defined programming line and therefore well identifiable from the outside. Developing an action of this type is perfectly legitimate, the question is whether the municipality should “pay for it”.

The municipality has two structuring projects in terms of cultural policy: Montolieu Village du Livre et des Arts and the Groupement d’Intérêt public La coopérative – Musée Cérès Franco. These two projects will necessarily require increased financial efforts in the years to come. Under these conditions, it believes that the municipality should focus on these two axes and that it can legitimately ask Le Rêve du Martinet, as to all other actors who intend to launch a cultural program, to approach the Association Montolieu Village du Livre et des Arts, and that it has no reason to disperse its aid and shatter its efforts. Laure ESCARÉ allows itself to recall that last March, during the vote on subsidies, it was clearly asked of “Le rêve du Martinet” to approach MVdL, arguing the approach made by the collective of the World Days of Art. The latter thus benefited from aid of 200 euros, to be paid to MVdL in addition to its own subsidy. There was no deliberation on the subsidy request.

The association has therefore taken the requested step, and discussions reveal that the position of Jeanne ETORÉ-LORTHOLARY is not shared by the president of MVdL.

L’argument avancé que la programmation de l’association ne sert pas le rayonnement culturel du village ne peut être tenu au seul prétexte que cette dernière ne fait pas apparaitre le sigle de MVdL, qui n’est pas une obligation. Le document remis à cet effet comporte en revanche le blason de la commune, ce qui le rattache donc clairement au village. [p.9](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/CRCM-27-05-2025-site-internet.pdf#page=9)

Laure ESCARÉ rappelle que le compte dédié aux dépenses de subventions est budgétisé à hauteur de 31 500 euros.

Le conseil lors du vote des subventions aux associations a consommé 31 275 euros. La commune dispose donc d’une marge de 225 euros, et elle peut parfaitement attribuer une aide de 100 euros, à l’association sans disperser ses efforts, comme le craint Jeanne ETORÉ- LORTHOLARY, si l’on compare cette somme à celle attribuée à MVdL.

Après échanges, le Conseil vote par 8 voix pour, 5 contre et une abstention l’attribution d’une subvention de 100 € à l’association le rêve du Martinet au titre de l’année 2025.

## Swimming pool market [p.10](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/CRCM-27-05-2025-site-internet.pdf#page=10)

As a reminder, the municipality has launched a contract for the refurbishment of the liners and filters of the swimming pool. To date, out of all the subsidies requested, only one response has been received by the secretariat, which does not allow for good visibility on the financing of all the operations. Furthermore, it is difficult to start the work in the autumn, as those of the church will begin, which will monopolize the cash flow, which will not allow for funds to finance other investment operations.

It is therefore proposed to the council to cancel the work contract on the swimming pool, without opening the bids, to allow it to be relaunched on the next financial year in full transparency with regard to the commitments of the companies, and with full knowledge of the subsidies granted.

The council unanimously decides to cancel the work contract concerning the swimming pool.
