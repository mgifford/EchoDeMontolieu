## Summary
The municipal council of Montolieu unanimously approved the minutes of its previous session and maintained local tax rates without increase. It adopted new rates for the municipal swimming pool, with a distinction between residents and non-residents, despite opposition on this point. The council voted subsidies to several associations and validated the financing of road works via the FPIC. Representatives were appointed for the inter-municipal syndicates.
