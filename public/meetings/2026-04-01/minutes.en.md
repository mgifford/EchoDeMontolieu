## Delegations of the municipal council to the Mayor

The Mayor may, by delegation of the municipal council, be charged, in whole or in part, and for the duration of his term of office: - To decide on the conclusion and revision of the lease of things for a period not exceeding twelve years; - To enter into insurance contracts as well as to accept the related claims indemnity; - To pronounce the granting and resumption of concessions in cemeteries; - To accept donations and legacies which are not burdened with conditions or charges; - To fix the resumption of alignments in application of a town planning document; - To authorise, on behalf of the municipality, the renewal of membership in associations of which it is a member; - To bring actions in justice in the name of the municipality or to defend the municipality in actions brought against it.

The proposed delegations are approved by a majority with 14 votes for and 1 vote against (Jeanne ÉTORÉ-LORTHOLARY) [p.3](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/CRCM-DU-01-avril-2026-site-internet.pdf#page=3)

## Establishment of the CCAS

The existence of the CCAS whose budget is funded by the municipal budget is an obligation. From its renewal, the municipal council has a maximum period of two months to proceed with the renewal of the CCAS board of directors (article R.123-10 of the code of social action and families).

It consists of 5 members elected within it, 5 external members appointed by the Mayor.
