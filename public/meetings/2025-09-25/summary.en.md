## Summary
The municipal council of 25 September 2025 approved its previous minutes, validated several equipment and development projects, particularly in the areas of video surveillance, mobility and pedestrian pathways. It also adopted social measures, such as participation in the financing of supplementary health insurance for staff, and gave its opinion on urban planning, solidarity and energy issues.
