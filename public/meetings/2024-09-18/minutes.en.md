## Project to install video protection

Faced with the increasing acts of incivility and degradation over the past two years, the municipality must provide itself with the means to react, to obtain redress, or to allow individuals to do so. Laure ESCARÉ recalls that a complaint against X has every chance of being closed without further action if the person responsible cannot be identified.

A representative from the gendarmerie travelled at the beginning of September to explain the principles of video protection for municipalities.

Based on an initial project for which a quote was established, it was recalled that the principle aims to film the public highway and thereby protect public buildings. In addition to specific equipment such as the swimming pool, or points where incivilities are multiplying, it is recommended to place cameras at the entrances to the village and at junctions.

A correspondent at the prefecture helps municipalities to calibrate their request, and to prepare their file. It is recalled that this is video protection, so there are no people monitoring the images continuously, but recordings that can be viewed upon requisition by the gendarmerie within the limit of a history set by the municipality (which can be 15 days, 3 weeks or one month). In order to continue the procedures and submit a file, it is requested that the council vote on the principle of implementing video protection.

The gendarmerie will be able to travel on request to meet with elected officials, and participate in a public meeting around this project.

- By a majority, the council votes by 10 votes for and 4 votes against, the implementation of a video

protection in the municipality.
