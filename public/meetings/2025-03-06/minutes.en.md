## Agreement with the SYADEN for a diagnosis of public lighting

Lors de sa séance du 24 novembre 2024, le Conseil municipal a autorisé la signature d’une convention avec un volet financier portant sur une opération de renforcement sur le réseau de distribution d’électricité, opération touchant également l’éclairage public, le tout dans le cadre des travaux sur le musée Cérès Franco. \_[not translated: an automatic check failed]\_

This agreement provides for a subsidy mitigating the cost of the operation for the town hall. The subsidy application requires the signature by the town hall of another agreement. Indeed, since the Syndical Committee of SYADEN in October 2021, in order to benefit from subsidies, municipalities must be registered with the SYADEN public lighting diagnostic mission.

This diagnosis includes: • A complete inventory of the municipality's lighting park; • The production of a georeferenced map in class A (in accordance with the DT/DICT reform) • A financial and energy assessment of the current installation; • A regulatory control of the installation; • A renovation master plan.

The cost of the mission for the municipality of Montolieu would be 3,309.60 € including VAT (including conditional tranches)

The draft agreement and the membership form were transmitted with the convocation to the council today.

The council votes unanimously - The municipality's membership in the public lighting diagnostic mission - Authorization to the Mayor to sign the agreement and the membership form - The registration of expenses in the 2025 budget, in the operating section. [p.4](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/CRCM-6-MARS-2025-site-internet.pdf#page=4)

## Pooling of walks with carcassonne agglo

By letter of February 19th, Mr. Régis Banquet reminds municipalities that they can benefit from pooled purchases by using permanent joint procurement groups, to which local authorities can adhere.

The procedure for awarding the contracts concerned is managed by the services of the Agglo, until their notification.

Then each member municipality ensures the execution of the contracts for its own needs: order and payment.

The list of contracts that will be relaunched or new contracts proposed is as follows: For the relaunched contracts

- Services of assistance and legal advice
- Topographical services
- Supply of fuel oil
- Supply of paper

### For the new contracts

- Services of periodic checks (electrical compliance, SSI, rescue equipment, areas of

play and sports equipment, machines, ladders, PPE, marquees, tents and structures, asbestos diagnosis before work, DPE, indoor air control ERP, RVRAT)

The municipalities have until March 17th to indicate if they are interested. According to feedback from participating municipalities, the advantage of the joint procurement is mainly the negotiated quality of the services, but the assistance and advice services, as well as the topographical services also allow for more competitive rates for small municipalities.

After discussion, the council votes unanimously for adhesion to the contracts

- Services of assistance and legal advice

## Demande d’augmentation de la participation de la commune au gip -musée céres franco

L’Assemblée générale du Groupement d’intérêt public qui administre La Coopérative- Musée Cérès Franco s’est tenue le 17 janvier.

L’objectif d’obtention du label « Musée de France » (qui permet de s’assurer le soutien de l’Etat par l’intermédiaire de la DRAC et garantit la sauvegarde des collections au sein des collections nationales quels que soient les aléas) et la réouverture en 2026 imposent un certain nombre d’exigences qui engendrent un coût et augmentent le budget prévisionnel de l’année 2025. Aussi a-t-il été demandé que la commune s’associe à l’effort financier de la région, de l’Agglo et d’un mécène privé et augmente sa participation au budget 2025 de 1000 à 2000 euros.
