## Modification of a deputy position

Following the resignation of Madame Agnès PAUTOU from the position of 4th deputy in charge of culture, heritage and tourism, we must decide whether or not to keep this deputy position.

The Mayor proposes to keep this position.

Vote of the municipal council unanimously

The Mayor proposes Mr. OLIVIER Jean-Luc to take the position of 4th deputy for culture, heritage and tourism.

Are there any other candidates? No

Mrs. ETORE-LORTHOLARY Jeanne informs the Municipal Council of a few small elements concerning this Deputy position: “Regarding the deputy position proposed to Jean-Luc OLIVIER, let’s recall a few small elements of history: [p.2](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/PV-CONSEIL-MUNICIPAL-DU-22-juillet-2026-site.pdf#page=2)

We requested this position after the elections. You preferred to entrust it to someone you knew perfectly well had little chance of assuming it. We thus lost six months.

You are appealing today to the one who was in second position on the list I led in the last elections.

I see this as proof of two things: - That you have, among your elected officials, no one competent in matters of tourism, culture or heritage, in a Village of Books and in such a beautiful village, it is really a shame;

\- That you hate me, I already know it, it saddens me, but it is also very unfortunate for everyone, and I don't think I deserve it so much.

I can only be delighted that you are calling on Jean-Luc Olivier, he is vice-president of the Association for the Enhancement of Heritage that I founded and that I chair, president of the Dry Stone and Capped Stones of Montolieu Association that I helped found and chaired for 12 years, president of the Friends of Saint-André Association, faithful member of the Montolieu Village of Books and Arts Association that I chaired for 20 years. He knows how to express himself, which is essential for a deputy for culture and which we have sorely lacked until now. He has a clear vision of financial problems and knows how to expose them with the same clarity.

And over the three mandates that we have lived or endured together, we have agreed on 90% of the questions. This shows how much I esteem and respect him, and that I a priori trust him to continue the action in the spirit that is ours.

Under the previous mandate, the deputy for c u l t u r e, tourism and heritage was a sort of non-existence who did absolutely nothing and who convened the corresponding commission once in six years.

There is therefore an urgent need for energetic and voluntary action, because we will not go further in economic development (which goes through tourism) without an enhancement of heritage and a cultural policy that is clearly visible and coordinated. I repeat, I trust Jean-Luc to take the measure of this challenge.”

The municipal council votes by majority for the election of Mr OLIVIER Jean-luc to the position of 4th Deputy for culture, heritage and tourism. 1 abstention
