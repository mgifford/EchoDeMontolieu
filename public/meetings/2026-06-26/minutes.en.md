## Public domain occupation fee: apartment n°2 Nursery school

The municipality wishes to rent apartment n°2 of the nursery school.

The Mayor explains that it is necessary to deliberate on the amount of the rent. The regulations indicate that contracts linking the municipality with its tenants when the apartment is attached to a public building are not leases but public domain occupation fees.

The unfurnished apartment, of type T3, is located on the 1st floor of the nursery school. It will be rented for a period of 6 years. The rents will be paid monthly and in arrears. A deposit of one month will be requested upon signature of the occupation fee contract. The Mayor proposes the price of 530.00€ with water included in the rent.

Council vote unanimously.
