## Schema d’amenagement et de gestion des eaux (sage) aude et cotiers audois

This scheme was announced at the last council meeting.

A development and management scheme for water (SAGE) is a local planning tool which organises a global and balanced management of water. It is driven by a local water commission (CLE) which brings together users of the resource. The objective is thus to ensure the balanced and sustainable management of the water resource, by reconciling satisfaction of the different uses and protection of the aquatic environments. It is based on a voluntary approach of consultation with local actors.

On September 11th last, Mr. the Prefect of the Aude transmitted the preliminary project in order to gather an opinion on the project perimeter, in accordance with articles L 212-3 and R 212-26 and following of the environmental code.

Without observation from the municipalities within the delay of 4 months from September 11th, the opinion will be deemed favourable.

The council gives unanimously a favourable opinion to the perimeter of the SAGE

## Territorial educational project of carcassonne agglo

Carcassonne Agglo wished to initiate an educational policy for children aged 0 to 17 years of the territory by the signature of a Territorial Educational Project (PEDT).

This project must allow to fight against educational, social and territorial inequalities, to respond

aux besoins des usagers (enfants et familles), de fédérer les différents acteurs du territoire, de donner un sens commun à l’action éducative, et de professionnalisation des agents. Ces enjeux se traduisent par 10 ambitions qui doivent favoriser la construction personnelle de l’enfant, son épanouissement et le vivre ensemble.
