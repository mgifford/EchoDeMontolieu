## Voting method

The Mayor invites the municipal council to proceed with the election of the delegates and their substitutes in view of the election of senators.

He recalls that, in application of articles L. 288 and R. 133 of the electoral code, the delegates and their substitutes are elected separately, without debate, by secret majority vote in two rounds. If mandates remain to be allocated after the first round of voting, which takes place with an absolute majority, a second round is held for the number of mandates remaining to be allocated and the election takes place with a relative majority. In the second round, in the event of a tie, the oldest candidate is declared elected.

In accordance with article L. 284 of the electoral code, if applicable, article L. 290-1 or L. 290-2, the municipal council must elect: 3 delegates and 3 substitutes. Candidates may run for election as delegates or substitutes, either individually, on an incomplete list, or on a list containing as many names as there are delegates or substitutes to be elected. Additions and deletions of names are authorised (art. L. 288 of the electoral code). The fact that a person has not stood for election or is absent does not prevent their election if they obtain the required number of votes.

## Creation of seasonal positions

Ms Agnès Pautou presents and explains the proposed choices:

The deliberation specifying the vote for the creation of seasonal positions for the municipality has not been found. This is mandatory to legitimise and legalise the hires. Furthermore, for several years, the contracts, working hours, job descriptions and organisation of work for seasonal workers have sometimes been limited or inconsistent. No seasonal worker had the same working hours and the hires as well as the positions were defined according to spontaneous requests.

It is not possible to respond to all requests, nor to choose seasonal workers without criteria or rules.

That is why it is important to reframe all of this, to create positions with a job description

and hiring criteria. [p.3](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/PV-CONSEIL-MUNICIPAL-DU-05-juin-2026.pdf#page=3)

The recruitments must be carried out according to the positions to be filled and not the other way around. For next year, a framed job offer will be published requesting adult people for the swimming pool and holding a B driving license for the technical service.

It is proposed to the municipal council to create non-permanent jobs to carry out the missions following the seasonal increase Accueil / swimming pool cash desk: 2 full-time positions per month Lifeguard: 2 full-time positions per month Technical service: 1 full-time position per month

## Loan application

The Mayor gives the floor to René Labessède, deputy for finance, to explain the choice of the loan to be contracted.

Following the preparation of the 2026 budget, the deficit of the investment budget after incorporation of the operating surplus (€26,000) is obliged to contract a loan of €225,000, due to a cumulative effect of this deficit in recent years for which no financial aid has been requested. Our request to three financial institutions was as follows: a loan of €225,000 in two parts: €125,000 over 15 years at a fixed rate and €100,000 over 15 years at a revisable rate (-1 point compared to the fixed rate). The revisable rate in order to be able to repay all or part of the capital in advance without financial penalty, with the sale of real estate belonging to the municipality. We also wish to benefit from a line of treasury of a small amount to ensure our treasury.

- Banque des territoires: it does not intervene on financing of the type line of
