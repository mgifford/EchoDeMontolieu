> This page is a hand-chosen list of official links; it does not use the minutes.

Free official maps and documents that help you understand a plot or a house before you look at it closely.

- [Géorisques: the risks at an address](https://www.georisques.gouv.fr/): The State’s official site. Type an address to see flooding, ground movement (clay shrink and swell, cavities, landslides), earthquake zone, radon and nearby industrial sites.
- [Montolieu’s local risk information document, DICRIM (PDF, French)](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/DICRIM_FR_Montolieu.pdf): The commune’s information document on major risks, published by the Mairie: what threatens the village and what to do.
- [The same document in English (PDF)](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/DICRIM_UK_Montolieu.pdf): English version published by the Mairie.
- [Municipal safeguard plan, citizen version (PDF, French)](https://www.montolieu.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/PCS-CITOYEN-MONTOLIEU.pdf): How the commune organises itself in a crisis (flood, storm, fire) and what residents should do.
- [InfoTerre (BRGM): geology maps](https://infoterre.brgm.fr/): The national geological survey. The maps show the soil and rock under a plot, useful to know whether a house stands on bedrock or on clay.
- [DVF map: recorded property sales](https://app.dvf.etalab.gouv.fr/): Property sales registered by the State, with date, price, surface and parcel. The data arrives some months after the sale.
- [Review of the local plan, PLU (Mairie)](https://www.montolieu.fr/vos-demarches/revision-du-plu/): Where the local planning document stands; it sets what can be built and where.
- [Planning and building permits (Mairie)](https://www.montolieu.fr/mairie/urbanisme/): The Mairie’s page on planning permissions and the rules that apply.

## Before buying

The seller must attach an up-to-date statement of risks and pollution for the property’s address to the sale contract. Ask for it early and compare it with Géorisques and the DICRIM.
